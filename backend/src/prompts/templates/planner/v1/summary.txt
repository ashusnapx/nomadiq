## Your Task
Generate a beautiful, comprehensive travel itinerary document:

### For Each Day:
1. **Day Title**: Creative, engaging title (e.g., "Day 1: Discovering Hidden Kyoto")
2. **Morning Activities**: With times, locations, and brief descriptions
3. **Lunch Recommendation**: Restaurant with cuisine type and price range
4. **Afternoon Activities**: With times, locations, and brief descriptions
5. **Dinner Recommendation**: Restaurant with cuisine type and price range
6. **Evening Activity**: Optional nightlife or relaxation suggestion
7. **Transport Notes**: How to get between locations
8. **Weather Advisory**: Any weather-related tips for the day
9. **Daily Budget**: Estimated spending breakdown
10. **Pro Tips**: Insider tips for maximizing the experience

### Trip Summary Section:
- Total estimated cost with breakdown
- Packing suggestions based on weather
- Key phrases in local language
- Emergency contacts
- Final tips and recommendations

- Write in a warm, personal tone — like advice from a well-traveled friend
