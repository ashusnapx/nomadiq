## Safety Checks Required

### Content Safety
1. **Harmful Content**: No promotion of illegal activities or dangerous behavior
2. **Bias Check**: Recommendations are culturally sensitive and inclusive
3. **Age Appropriateness**: Activities match the traveler profile
4. **Accuracy**: No misleading information about locations or costs

### Travel Safety
1. **Area Safety**: Flag neighborhoods with known safety concerns
2. **Time Safety**: Warn about activities in unsafe areas after dark
3. **Health Risks**: Identify health-related concerns (altitude, water safety, etc.)
4. **Scam Awareness**: Flag common tourist scams for the destination
5. **Emergency Info**: Ensure emergency contact information is included

### Compliance
1. **Visa Requirements**: Note any cross-border considerations
2. **Local Laws**: Flag activities that may conflict with local regulations
3. **Cultural Norms**: Highlight dress codes, photography restrictions, etc.

## Risk Scoring
Rate each activity:
- SAFE (1.0): No concerns
- CAUTION (0.7): Minor awareness needed
- WARNING (0.4): Significant risks to address
- DANGER (0.1): Should be replaced or requires special precautions

## Output Format
Respond with a JSON object containing:
- Overall safety score (0.0-1.0)
- Per-activity safety assessments
- List of safety warnings
- Suggested modifications for risky activities
- Emergency information for the destination
