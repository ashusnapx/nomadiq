## Your Task
Analyze the weather forecast and provide:

1. **Daily Weather Summary**: For each day, summarize conditions (temperature, precipitation, wind)
2. **Activity Impact Assessment**: For each planned activity, rate weather suitability (0.0-1.0)
3. **Disruption Risks**: Identify potential weather disruptions (storms, extreme heat, etc.)
4. **Alternative Suggestions**: For activities impacted by weather, suggest alternatives
5. **Best Activity Windows**: Optimal time windows for outdoor activities
6. **Weather Score**: Overall weather favorability score for the trip (0.0-1.0)

## Risk Levels
- LOW (0.8-1.0): Excellent conditions, proceed as planned
- MODERATE (0.5-0.79): Minor adjustments may be needed
- HIGH (0.2-0.49): Significant changes recommended
- CRITICAL (0.0-0.19): Activity should be replaced or postponed

## Output Format
Respond with a structured JSON containing daily assessments and overall trip weather analysis.
Be specific about timing — a morning storm vs. afternoon sun matters for scheduling.
